What do these signals mean?

Continuity

Measures the smoothness and persistence of price movement. High continuity indicates stable directional flow; low continuity indicates noise or chop.

Trend

Captures the directional bias of the market. Positive trend indicates upward pressure; negative trend indicates downward pressure.

Acceleration

Measures the rate of change of the trend. Positive acceleration suggests strengthening momentum; negative acceleration suggests weakening momentum.

Regime

A structural classification of market state (Bullish, Bearish, Neutral, or Transitional) based on continuity, trend, acceleration, and volatility.

Collapse Score

A risk indicator that highlights instability or breakdown conditions in the underlying structure of the price series.
